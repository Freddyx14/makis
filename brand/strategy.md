# Mark AI — Brand strategy

> Working identity for the product formerly named Makis. Version 1.0 · 2026-09-12

## Brief

Mark AI is the operating environment for the founder of a marketing agency. It connects the work that is usually fragmented across commercial, delivery, finance, legal, campaigns and team capacity, then gives the founder one place to ask, decide and approve.

The mark must feel useful at 09:00 on a difficult Monday: clear enough to reveal a risk, warm enough not to feel like enterprise software, and precise enough to guide real action. Orange and white are the deliberate signature. The product should not look like another blue AI dashboard.

## Audience

1. **Agency founder.** Owns client relationships, cash, quality and priorities. Needs a reliable overview without becoming the operator of six separate tools.
2. **Functional lead.** Runs campaigns, delivery, commercial or finance. Needs a focused module with client context, guardrails and a clear handoff back to the founder.

## Positioning

For agency founders who run their company across disconnected tools, Mark AI is an agentic operating environment that connects business context to the next decision, because its departments, documents and agents work from the same client reality.

## Core insight

The problem is not that founders lack dashboards. It is that context is distributed while accountability remains concentrated in one person.

## Brand decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Orange + white, with near-black ink | A memorable high-contrast system that avoids the default blue AI category cue and keeps working surfaces calm. |
| Sage primary, Creator influencer | The product makes complicated work legible, then helps a team make and ship work. |
| Modular “M” symbol | A single connected mark made of four operational blocks. It scales to a favicon and can become a UI motif. |
| Direct, operational language | The product earns trust through traceable context and clear next actions, not hype. |
| Branded house | Departments are Mark AI Commercial, Mark AI Finance, Mark AI Legal, Mark AI Delivery and Mark AI Campaigns. |

## Product proof to build toward

- A founder can ask what needs attention and land on the source document or action.
- A client enters once and becomes usable by commercial, delivery, legal, finance and campaigns.
- Every proposed external action retains human approval and evidence.

## Competitive frame

Mark AI does not compete on being a generic AI chat, a project board, a CRM, or a standalone campaign manager. Its category is the connected operating environment for an agency founder.
