# Add company foundation to the Mark AI PRD

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Freddy Ñañez  
**Area:** Platform

## What changed

Expanded the PRD with canonical company strategy, company data, goals, KPIs, people, roles, capacity, and hiring artifacts.

## Why it changed

Agency founders need to manage the company itself, not only individual clients, campaigns, and operational tasks.

## User-visible behavior

The future founder cockpit can surface strategic goals, KPI quality, role ownership, workload and hiring needs alongside commercial, delivery, finance, legal and campaign context.

## Data, permissions, or external effects

People information is deliberately limited to professional role, responsibilities, capability, availability and workload. The product does not require or model sensitive HR records.
